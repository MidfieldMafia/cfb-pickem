GOOD TO KNOW
Changing picks

Change any pick, your Lock or your guess until the Deadline. A Lock that loses costs nothing.

Missing a week

A week with no picks doesn't count. You score nothing, and your record is untouched.

Canceled games

A canceled or postponed game counts for no one. A Lock on it drops, and you can move it before the Deadline.

More than one group

You see one group at a time. Your picks count in every group you're in.

Making picks

Pick the winner of every game. Straight up, no spread.

Deadline

Everything locks at the week's first kickoff. You can change picks until then.

Lock of the Week

{{lock}}× points on one pick you mark. Nothing lost if it misses.

Tiebreaker Guess

Guess the total points in one marked game. If the week is tied, closest guess wins it.

Reveal

No one sees your picks before the Deadline. After it, everyone sees everyone's.

Scoring

{{points}} points for every correct pick.

Top score in your group wins the week. Season ties go to total points, then weekly wins, then Tiebreaker Guesses.

Missing a week

A week with no picks doesn't count. You score nothing, and your record is untouched.

Canceled games

A canceled or postponed game counts for no one. A Lock on it drops, and you can move it before the Deadline.

Your groups

You see one group at a time. Your picks count in every group you're in.

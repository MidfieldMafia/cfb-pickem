End zone colours · three rules

Bar pair

barColors as the split bar uses it. Only colours that read on the cream card count, so a gold or bright orange gives way to gray.

Not like the turf

Any school colour that reads apart from the pine. The name turns cream or ink to suit. Two alike colours: the away side gives way to chalk.

Primary always

Each school’s primary, no pairing. The end zones are a field apart and named, so two oranges never touch.

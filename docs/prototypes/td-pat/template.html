Should the kick be its own beat?

Three copies of the Game sheet's field, on one clock, playing the same recorded 58-yard Liberty touchdown at Coastal. Pick the try, press Replay, or drag the bar to stop on any moment. A is the app today. B and C clear the touchdown's line when the kick starts, which is Jonah's view.

What to decide

  • A, B or C. A keeps the run and the kick on the field together, at rest too. B wipes the run the instant the ball sets for the kick. C lets the touchdown sit for a beat before it wipes, then sets the ball, then kicks.
  • The end zone. Whether TOUCHDOWN stays lit while the try is in the air (the checkbox), or goes dark with the run's line.
  • At rest. Once the kick lands, B and C leave only the kick's arc (and the X on a miss). The touchdown's line is gone until the next snap.

Each drawing comes from the app's own describePlays and timeline (src/lib/results/field.ts, field-player.ts), run on the recorded feed. Only B's and C's clearing and C's pause are new.

Each card is a 360px phone with the field drawn as field-card.tsx draws it today, plus the end zones from design A of “End zones and play labels”. The field paints the same in both themes; only the card around it changes. No source gives a turf hex, so each turf has three shades picked by eye from photos.

What to decide

  • The turf shade for each of Boise State, Coastal Carolina and Eastern Michigan.
  • Whether any marking or overlay changes colour on a coloured turf. “Tuned per turf” is one answer: it lifts the chalk on gray and turns Coastal's teal end zone to black, as the real field has since 2025.
  • Whether LSU's numbers every 5 yards stay readable at this width, or need a smaller face.

The table under each card gives the contrast of every mark against that turf. Under 3:1 is shown in red. Pine is the bar to meet, not 3:1. On pine the line to gain is 3.2:1 and the end zones sit near 1:1, held apart by the chalk goal line.

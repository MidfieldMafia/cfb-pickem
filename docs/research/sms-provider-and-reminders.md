# SMS Provider & Reminder Scheduling for Saturday Slate

## Summary

For a private 10-25 person hobby pick'em app, the fastest realistic path to sending real texts is **Twilio with a toll-free number and Toll-Free Verification**, not standard 10DLC. Twilio's own docs put Toll-Free Verification at roughly **3-5 business days** ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5377174717595-Toll-Free-Message-Verification-for-US-Canada)), it's free ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5377174717595-Toll-Free-Message-Verification-for-US-Canada)), and it avoids the multi-week uncertainty of standard 10DLC brand/campaign vetting. Standard 10DLC campaign vetting is quoted by Twilio as "about 7 business days" ([support.twilio.com search index](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/11847054539547-A2P-10DLC-Campaign-Approval-Requirements)) but Twilio's own Sole Proprietor path (the cheapest/simplest 10DLC option for a no-EIN hobby project) warns approval "might take several weeks" due to high volume ([twilio.com/docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc/direct-sole-proprietor-registration-overview)) — worse, not better, than toll-free. Telnyx offers essentially the same toll-free timeline (~5 business days or less, per [support.telnyx.com](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/10729979-toll-free-verification-request-guide)) at a lower per-message price, and is a reasonable second choice. AWS End User Messaging is the slowest and most complex of the group — its own docs cite up to 4 weeks for 10DLC campaign approval and up to 15 business days for toll-free ([docs.aws.amazon.com](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registration-eta.html)) — so it should be avoided for a project on any kind of near-term timeline. Free carrier email-to-SMS gateways (vtext.com, txt.att.net) are not a viable option: AT&T's gateway is already shut down and Verizon's is scheduled for shutdown by March 2027 with reliability already degraded. **Bottom line: sign up for Twilio, buy a toll-free number, submit Toll-Free Verification same day, and expect to be sending real texts to the whole family group in about 3-5 business days** — which does fit inside a "within a week" expectation, though not within 3 days in the best case, and not before verification completes because Twilio blocks unverified toll-free sending outright as of Jan 31, 2024. Scheduling reminders is straightforward: Vercel Hobby's cron is capped at once/day with imprecise timing, so pair it with the free, minute-granularity cron-job.org (or GitHub Actions, 5-minute granularity) hitting a `CRON_SECRET`-protected Route Handler for anything that needs same-day precision.

## Twilio

### 10DLC long code (standard path)
- A2P 10DLC registration is a two/three-step process: create a Business Profile, register a US A2P Brand, then register a Campaign use case describing the messaging purpose via The Campaign Registry ([twilio.com/docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc)).
- Brand registration can typically complete "in a few minutes" but may need up to 24 hours, and if secondary vetting is triggered that can take up to 7 days ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405758341659-A2P-10DLC-Brand-Approval-Best-Practices)).
- Standard campaign registrations go through carrier vetting that "currently takes about 7 business days" ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/11847054539547-A2P-10DLC-Campaign-Approval-Requirements)).
- 10DLC numbers that are not registered receive additional carrier surcharges and heavier message filtering ([twilio.com/docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc)).

### Toll-free (alternative sending path)
- Toll-Free Verification is required to send SMS/MMS over a toll-free number to US/Canada; as of January 31, 2024, unverified toll-free senders are blocked outright ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5377174717595-Toll-Free-Message-Verification-for-US-Canada)).
- The verification process "can take approximately 3-5 business days for completion" and Twilio "offers this verification service free of cost" ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5377174717595-Toll-Free-Message-Verification-for-US-Canada)).
- Verified senders "should see greatly reduced message filtering" across major US/Canada networks ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5377174717595-Toll-Free-Message-Verification-for-US-Canada)).
- Submissions go through the Console or the Messaging Compliance API ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5377174717595-Toll-Free-Message-Verification-for-US-Canada)).

### Low-volume / sole proprietor 10DLC path
- Twilio offers a **Sole Proprietor** brand/campaign type specifically for individuals or hobbyists in the US/Canada without a business EIN or Canadian Business Number, filing under a personal SSN/SIN ([twilio.com/docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc/direct-sole-proprietor-registration-overview)).
- Sole Proprietor Campaigns are restricted to **one 10DLC phone number per campaign**, and carry lower throughput/volume ceilings than Standard brands ([twilio.com/docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc/direct-sole-proprietor-registration-overview)).
- Brand approval for this path "typically occurs within a few minutes," but campaign vetting "might take several weeks" due to high submission volume ([twilio.com/docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc/direct-sole-proprietor-registration-overview)).
- A separate **Low-Volume Standard** brand/campaign type exists for entities that *do* have a Tax ID/EIN but send under roughly 6,000 segments/day and need low throughput; when a Sole Proprietor's needs grow or it acquires an EIN, Twilio requires transitioning to Standard or Low-Volume Standard ([twilio.com/docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc/transition-sole-proprietor-to-standard-brand)).

### Costs (Twilio)
- Per-message send price to US numbers: $0.0083 per SMS segment (same rate for long code and toll-free), plus a pass-through carrier fee that varies by destination carrier (e.g., AT&T carrier fee $0.0035) ([twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/us](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/us)).
- Phone number monthly rental: local/10DLC number $1.15/month; toll-free number $2.15/month ([twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/us](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/us)).
- A2P Brand registration one-time fee: a base $4.50 TCR registration fee applies to all Brands, plus an additional Standard Brand vetting charge of $41.50 for Standard (non-sole-proprietor, non-low-volume) Brands ([twilio.com/docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/trust-hub/registrations/a2p-10dlc-brand)). Newer reporting on Twilio's own pricing shows Low-Volume Standard brand registration reduced to a $4 fee ([search results citing twilio.com/docs/trust-hub/registrations/a2p-10dlc-brand](https://www.twilio.com/docs/trust-hub/registrations/a2p-10dlc-brand)) — confirm exact current figure in the Twilio Console before registering, since these fees have changed more than once.
- Campaign registration: Twilio charges monthly campaign fees to maintain the campaign record, with the exact amount varying by use case; Twilio directs customers to its dedicated A2P 10DLC fees article for the current breakdown ([twilio.com/docs/trust-hub/registrations/a2p-10dlc-campaign](https://www.twilio.com/docs/trust-hub/registrations/a2p-10dlc-campaign) → [help.twilio.com/articles/1260803965530](https://help.twilio.com/articles/1260803965530)). Reported figures for low-volume/low-throughput campaign types are roughly $1.50-$10/month plus a one-time ~$15 external vetting fee ([twilio.com/docs/trust-hub/registrations/a2p-10dlc-campaign](https://www.twilio.com/docs/trust-hub/registrations/a2p-10dlc-campaign)) — again, verify the exact current number in-console since Twilio periodically restructures A2P pricing.
- Toll-Free Verification itself has no fee ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5377174717595-Toll-Free-Message-Verification-for-US-Canada)); you still pay the $2.15/month number rental and per-message rate above.
- Twilio's free trial gives a small starting balance but restricts outbound SMS to at most **5 manually-verified phone numbers**, and upgrading (adding a payment method) is required to send to unverified numbers ([twilio.com/docs/usage/tutorials/how-to-use-your-free-trial-account](https://www.twilio.com/docs/usage/tutorials/how-to-use-your-free-trial-account)) — meaning a 10-25 person group requires an upgraded (paid) account regardless of registration path.

### Link/content restrictions
- Twilio explicitly advises against using shared public URL shorteners (e.g., free TinyURL or Bitly links) in US SMS/MMS; carrier policy discourages them because they're frequently abused by spammers, and filtered messages using them have no recourse ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/1260804572090-How-can-I-send-shortened-links-in-my-messages)).
- Twilio's own error code 30525 ("Toll Free verification rejection - Public URL shorteners not allowed") confirms that a toll-free verification submission will be rejected outright if it uses a public shortener domain ([twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30525](https://www.twilio.com/docs/api/errors/30525)).
- Twilio recommends a dedicated, business-branded short domain (via its own Link Shortening feature) rather than a shared shortener if links must be sent ([twilio.com/docs/messaging/features/link-shortening](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/features/link-shortening)).
- Practical takeaway for Saturday Slate: if reminder texts include a link to the app, use the app's own domain directly rather than any bit.ly/tinyurl-style shortener.

## Alternatives

### Telnyx
- Registration requirements: Telnyx implements the same carrier-mandated 10DLC brand/campaign and toll-free verification rules as Twilio, exposed through its own Mission Control Portal and API ([developers.telnyx.com](https://developers.telnyx.com/docs/messaging/10dlc/quickstart)), and it offers a comparable Sole Proprietor 10DLC registration flow ([developers.telnyx.com](https://developers.telnyx.com/docs/messaging/10dlc/sole-proprietor/index)).
- Lead time: Telnyx quotes 10DLC brand vetting at roughly 1-7 business days and campaign carrier approval also around 1-7 business days ([developers.telnyx.com](https://developers.telnyx.com/docs/messaging/10dlc/brand-registration)); toll-free verification approvals are "normally 5 business days or less" per Telnyx's help center, though it can be faster or slower depending on submission volume ([support.telnyx.com](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/10729979-toll-free-verification-request-guide)). Telnyx also notes toll-free registration is generally the "faster, cheaper, and easier" compliance path relative to 10DLC for its customers ([telnyx.com](https://telnyx.com/resources/10dlc-vs-toll-free-isvs)).
- Cost: Outbound SMS via 10DLC is $0.004 per message part; via toll-free it's $0.0055 per message part, both plus pass-through carrier fees ([telnyx.com/pricing/messaging](https://telnyx.com/pricing/messaging)) — noticeably cheaper per-message than Twilio's $0.0083. Toll-free number registration itself is free ([telnyx.com](https://telnyx.com/resources/10dlc-vs-toll-free-isvs)).
- Free tier: Telnyx does not run an ongoing general free-credit trial; it periodically offers a no-credit-card "Pretrial" account for specific products and a much larger credit package through its Startups Program, but no standing small free-SMS-credit trial equivalent to Twilio's was found on Telnyx's own site as of this research ([support.telnyx.com](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/14327893-telnyx-pretrial-accounts)).

### AWS End User Messaging (formerly SNS SMS)
- Registration requirements: AWS enforces the same industry-wide 10DLC brand/campaign registration and toll-free verification, done through the AWS End User Messaging (or classic SNS) console ([docs.aws.amazon.com](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registrations-10dlc.html)).
- Lead time: AWS's own documentation states 10DLC campaign registration is expected to take **up to 4 weeks** ([docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registration-eta.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registration-eta.html)), and toll-free number registration "can take up to 15 business days" ([docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registration-eta.html](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registration-eta.html)) — both are meaningfully slower than Twilio or Telnyx's toll-free path.
- Cost: 10DLC company/brand registration is a $4 one-time fee; 10DLC campaign registration carries a $50 one-time fee (currently waived); monthly campaign fees are $10/month for a regular campaign or $2/month for a low-volume campaign; each 10DLC phone number is $1/month ([aws.amazon.com/sns/sms-pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/sns/sms-pricing/)). Toll-free-originated messages to US destinations carry an additional $0.0025 carrier fee per message on top of the base send rate ([aws.amazon.com/sns/sms-pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/sns/sms-pricing/)).
- Free tier: the first 100 SMS messages sent to US destinations each month are included in the AWS Free Tier and this allowance does not expire ([aws.amazon.com/sns/sms-pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/sns/sms-pricing/)) — plenty for a 10-25 person weekly reminder use case cost-wise, though new accounts start in an SMS "sandbox" mode limited to verified numbers until a payment method is added and the account exits sandbox.

### Email-to-SMS carrier gateways (not recommended)
- This is not a paid API — it means emailing `number@carrier-domain.com` and having the carrier convert it to a text, with no A2P registration involved, but it is being actively phased out.
- AT&T's txt.att.net / mms.att.net (and Cricket's equivalent txt.cricket.net) email-to-text gateway was discontinued as of June 17, 2025; messages sent to these addresses are silently dropped with no delivery and no bounce ([reported based on AT&T's own discontinuation notice, referenced across carrier-status trackers](https://simplifiedalerts.com/att-email-to-text/)).
- Verizon's vtext.com/vzwpix.com gateway has been announced for full shutdown by March 31, 2027, per Verizon's own support notice, and is already unreliable due to increased spam filtering in the interim ([verizon.com/support/vtext-vzwpix-shutdown](https://www.verizon.com/support/vtext-vzwpix-shutdown/)).
- Given both major US carriers have discontinued or are actively sunsetting this mechanism, and delivery is already unreliable, email-to-SMS gateways should not be used for Saturday Slate.

## Speed comparison

Direct answer to "can any option plausibly be sending real texts to real US phones within 3-5 days of signing up?": **Yes — but only via the toll-free verification path, on Twilio or Telnyx.**

- **Twilio toll-free**: buy a toll-free number, submit Toll-Free Verification same day, approval in ~3-5 business days per Twilio's own docs ([support.twilio.com](https://support.twilio.com/hc/en-us/articles/5377174717595-Toll-Free-Message-Verification-for-US-Canada)). This is the single fastest realistic, well-documented path for a small hobby app.
- **Telnyx toll-free**: comparable, "5 business days or less" per Telnyx ([support.telnyx.com](https://support.telnyx.com/en/articles/10729979-toll-free-verification-request-guide)), at a lower per-message rate — a good second choice, especially if already comfortable with a less mainstream provider's console/API.
- **Twilio/Telnyx 10DLC (Sole Proprietor or Standard)**: not reliably fast. Twilio quotes ~7 business days for standard campaign vetting, and explicitly warns Sole Proprietor campaigns can take "several weeks" ([twilio.com/docs](https://www.twilio.com/docs/messaging/compliance/a2p-10dlc/direct-sole-proprietor-registration-overview)). Telnyx's 10DLC path (1-7 business days) is faster than Twilio's Sole Proprietor path but still not as predictable as either vendor's toll-free process.
- **AWS End User Messaging**: the slowest of all options researched — up to 4 weeks for 10DLC campaigns and up to 15 business days even for toll-free ([docs.aws.amazon.com](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/sms-voice/latest/userguide/registration-eta.html)) — not a fit for a near-term timeline.
- **Email-to-SMS gateways**: technically "instant" with no registration, but not a real option given AT&T's gateway is already dead and Verizon's is degrading toward a 2027 shutdown.

**Fastest realistic path for this 10-25 person hobby app: Twilio, toll-free number + Toll-Free Verification, submitted on day one.** Expect real texts flowing to the full family group in about 3-5 business days, not 3-5 calendar days, so plan for roughly a week end-to-end once you include buying the number, submitting the form, and Twilio's queueing/processing.

## Vercel Cron & Scheduling

- **Hobby plan**: up to 100 cron jobs per project, but the minimum interval is **once per day**; more-frequent cron expressions fail at deploy time, and even the one daily run is imprecise — Vercel invokes it "at any point within the specified hour" (e.g., a 1:00am job can fire anytime between 1:00am and 1:59am) ([vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/usage-and-pricing](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/usage-and-pricing)).
- **Pro plan** (and Enterprise): also 100 cron jobs per project, but the minimum interval drops to **once per minute**, with per-minute scheduling precision (invocation guaranteed within the specified minute) ([vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/usage-and-pricing](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/usage-and-pricing)).
- Cron jobs are billed as regular Vercel Functions invocations — no separate cron fee on any plan ([vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/usage-and-pricing](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/usage-and-pricing)).
- Vercel's own docs also warn that cron delivery is "best effort": invocations can occasionally be missed or duplicated, so cron-triggered endpoints should be written to be idempotent ([vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/manage-cron-jobs](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/manage-cron-jobs)).

**Free external cron fallbacks** (for sub-daily precision on the Hobby plan):
- [cron-job.org](https://cron-job.org/en/) — entirely free, supports up to 60 executions/hour (i.e., every minute), no hard cap on number of jobs, 30-second execution timeout and 64KB response cap per job ([cron-job.org/en/faq](https://cron-job.org/en/faq/)).
- GitHub Actions scheduled workflows — free on public repos (and included minutes on private repos), minimum interval of 5 minutes per GitHub's own docs, though the `schedule` event "can be delayed during periods of high load" (notably the top of every hour) and, on public repos, scheduled workflows auto-disable after 60 days of repo inactivity ([docs.github.com](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/writing-workflows/choosing-when-your-workflow-runs/events-that-trigger-workflows)).

**Verifying requests are genuinely from Vercel Cron**: Vercel's docs recommend setting a `CRON_SECRET` environment variable (a random string, 16+ characters) in the project. Vercel automatically sends this value as an `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>` header on every cron invocation, and the Route Handler should compare the incoming header against the stored secret before doing any work, returning 401 on mismatch ([vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/manage-cron-jobs](https://vercel.com/docs/cron-jobs/manage-cron-jobs)). This same pattern should be reused for any external cron fallback (cron-job.org, GitHub Actions) by having it send the same secret as a bearer token or custom header, so the app can trust either scheduler equally.

The rule. A logo sits inside the disc at 72% with object-fit: contain, over the existing 18% tint of the school's colors.primary. A flag keeps bleeding past the edge at 125%. No crop, no second shape, no small-size fallback.
Accepted, not overlooked: a light primary makes a very faint disc — Iowa is #FFCD00 — and an inset logo at 20px is small. Both were looked at with the alternatives drawn and judged fine. Do not add a border or a tint floor to "fix" the faint disc.

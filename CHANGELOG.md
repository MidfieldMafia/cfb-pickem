# Changelog

What has changed in Saturday Slate, newest first.

**What's new** is written for the people playing: the handful of things a member
would notice, in their words. **All changes** is every pull request that landed,
generated by `npm run changelog` — do not edit it by hand.


## What's new

<!-- whats-new:start -->

### 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-21

- You now play in a group. The family is in Mabry Family, and anyone can start a group of their own and bring people in with a Join Link.
- Your name at the top opens the You screen: switch between your groups, copy your personal link, or leave a group.
- The Leaderboard sorts by any column, hands the trophy to a sole leader, and can show one week at a time.
- The Live Board shows possession, down and distance, and the last play while games are running.
- Text yourself your sign-in link instead of digging for the email.
- A week you make no picks in no longer drags your average down. It simply doesn't count.
- Rules is now How to play, and it comes up the first time you set yourself up.
- Eight new pennants to choose from, and eight nobody had picked have retired.
- Opening the app from your Home Screen shows the Saturday Slate launch screen instead of a white flash.
- History opens on the most recent week's results rather than Week 1.

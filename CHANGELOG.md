# Changelog

What has changed in Saturday Slate, newest first.

**What's new** is written for the people playing: the handful of things a member
would notice, in their words. **All changes** is every pull request that landed,
generated by `npm run changelog` — do not edit it by hand.


## What's new

<!-- whats-new:start -->

### 2026-09-22 to 2026-09-27

- Chat is a new tab for your Group. React to a message with Flag, Hot take, Respect or Ha, and delete your own messages if you think better of them.
- History now lives on the Leaderboard. A strip across the top takes you to the Season or any Played Week, and while a week's games are still going it opens on that week.
- Every finished week has its own Reveal: your picks and everyone else's, game by game, a tap from that week's standings.
- Tap a game on the Live Board for its game sheet: kickoff time, TV, venue, weather, the spread and your own pick before it starts; the latest plays and the last drive while it's on; team stats and game leaders once it's final.
- Your Pennant can now be your team's logo, or a photo of you. Change it from the You screen.
- Send the commissioners a bug or an idea, screenshot and all, from Send feedback in the Profile menu or on the You screen.
- The Season table fits on a phone. Long names shorten instead of pushing the numbers off the screen, and Win% takes the place of the old record and average.
- You can jump to Review from any game on your picks, so setting your Lock of the Week and Tiebreaker Guess no longer means picking every game first.
- Team names fit on one line on your picks, and logos no longer crowd the rank and the check mark.
- Spreads and rankings keep updating every day until each game kicks off, even after the slate is out.
- The Weekly Win badge waits until the week is decided, and a week nobody in your Group picked no longer shows up as an empty one.
- What's new now opens by itself once when there's something new, and stays in the Profile menu after that.

### 2026-09-15 to 2026-09-21

- You now play in a group. The family is in Mabry Family, and anyone can start a group of their own and bring people in with a Join Link.
- Your name at the top opens the You screen: switch between your groups, copy your personal link, or leave a group.
- The Leaderboard sorts by any column, hands the trophy to a sole leader, and can show one week at a time.
- The Live Board shows possession, down and distance, and the last play while games are running.
- Text yourself your sign-in link instead of digging for the email.
- A week you make no picks in no longer drags your average down. It simply doesn't count.
- Rules is now How to play, and it comes up the first time you set yourself up.
- Eight new pennants to choose from, and eight nobody had picked have retired.
- Opening the app from your Home Screen shows the Saturday Slate launch screen instead of a white flash.
- History opens on the most recent week's results rather than Week 1.
